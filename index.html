About

I have spent the last nine years shipping machine learning to production, the last four of them focused on large language models. I work across the whole path: data, retrieval, prompting and fine-tuning, serving, and the evaluation harness that tells you whether any of it got better.

I care most about the unglamorous parts that decide whether an AI feature survives contact with real users: regression evals, graceful fallbacks, observability, and a clear number for what each request costs.
